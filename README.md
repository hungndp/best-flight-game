**AZURE REACH — 12 missions · 6 aircraft · 5 airfields · 40 rings · 3 storm fronts.** An island-hopper flight campaign across the Meridian Isles: a complete 2D canvas flight game that plays offline in a desktop browser from static files, keyboard + mouse, no network requests. Chase view over recognisable island terrain, the aircraft in frame with instruments lit, runways and target rings ahead, and altitude, speed and heading on the HUD.

Fly twelve missions with different goals — ring courses, mail deliveries, rescues, storm-front crossings, a night landing by instruments, a banner tow and the Meridian Grand Tour. Fuel and damage turn every sortie into decisions; throttle discipline is scored. Earn Sky Credits in the air and spend them in the hangar: six aircraft to unlock, twelve upgrades and liveries, three daily contracts seeded by the date, eight pilot ranks and a logbook that remembers every flight.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and fly. Controls: arrows / WASD fly, Shift / Ctrl throttle, Space airbrake, Esc menu, M mute, 1–4 preview the four screenshot scenes from the title screen.
